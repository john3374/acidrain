# Acid Rain

A 90s-style typing game: words fall from the top of the screen and the player types them before they land.

## Language

**Game**:
One play-through, from starting at a chosen level until the player quits or runs out of life.
_Avoid_: Round, session, match

**Score**:
The final points from one finished Game.
_Avoid_: Record, result, 점수 entry

**Leaderboard**:
A ranked list of Scores within one Period.
_Avoid_: Scoreboard, ranking, high-score table

**Period**:
The time window a Leaderboard covers: today, the last 7 days, the last 30 days, or all time.
_Avoid_: Range, window, tab
